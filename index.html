What We Do

Six services across the capital project lifecycle.

Engineering and project services for regulated manufacturing, engaged individually or as an integrated scope, and scaled from a single owner's engineer to an embedded project team.

  1. 01Project Engineering & ManagementOwner-side engineering and project management from kickoff through closeout.
  2. 02Conceptual & Detailed DesignProcess and mechanical design from basis of design through construction-ready packages.
  3. 03Construction Administration & OversightField coordination and verification that what's built matches what was designed.
  4. 04Commissioning, Qualification & ValidationPlanning and execution from commissioning plan through IQ, OQ, and PQ.
  5. 05Change, Risk & Compliance ManagementStructured change control, living risk registers, and inspection-ready documentation.
  6. 06Process Optimization & Tech TransferMoving and improving processes across scales, suites, and sites, and supporting them after startup.

Industries

Where we work

Pharmaceutical

Oral solid dose, sterile fill-finish, inhalation, and API facilities operating under cGMP, where documentation rigor, contamination control, and inspection readiness shape every engineering decision.

Biotechnology

Upstream and downstream bioprocessing on single-use and stainless platforms, and the clean utilities and controlled environments that support them.

Advanced Manufacturing

High-precision, highly regulated production environments, including medical device manufacturing, where uptime, traceability, and controlled change are non-negotiable.
