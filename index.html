Delivery Model

The capital project lifecycle

We engage at any stage — but the earlier we're involved, the more risk and cost we can design out before it becomes rework.

  1. Phase 01

    Concept

    Business case, capacity basis, and early feasibility.

  2. Phase 02

    Design

    Basis of design, URS, and front-end engineering.

  3. Phase 03

    Procurement

    Vendor selection, FAT planning, and long-lead management.

  4. Phase 04

    Construction

    Field oversight, coordination, and progress verification.

  5. Phase 05

    CQV

    Commissioning, qualification, and validation execution.

  6. Phase 06

    Handover

    GMP-ready turnover and continuous improvement.
