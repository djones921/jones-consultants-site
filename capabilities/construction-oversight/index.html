Construction Administration and Oversight for Pharmaceutical and Biotech Facilities

Drawings describe intent; construction is where intent meets a jobsite. JCS Engineering provides owner-side construction administration for pharmaceutical, biotech, and advanced manufacturing facilities: managing the submittal and RFI flow, walking the work, verifying progress and pay applications, reviewing change orders, and sequencing construction around operations that cannot stop.

Scope of service

  • Document flow. Submittal and RFI logs, technical review and turnaround tracking, and coordination between the A/E, contractor, and equipment vendors so questions are answered once, on the record.
  • Field verification. Regular walkdowns against drawings and specifications, observation reports, and early identification of deviations before they are covered up or built upon.
  • Commercial control. Progress verification against the schedule and pay applications, change order review for scope, cost, schedule, and compliance impact, and an owner-side recommendation on each.
  • Operations protection. Shutdown planning, pressure-cascade and containment controls, tie-in sequencing, and communication with production and quality so construction inside or adjacent to a GMP area does not become a deviation.
  • Completion. Mechanical completion walkdowns, punch classification by criticality, punch closure and escalation, and review of turnover packages before acceptance.

How we work

We attend the owner-architect-contractor meetings, own the owner's action items, and report weekly on progress, open RFIs, change exposure, and risk. Field presence scales with the phase — heavier during tie-ins, equipment set, and mechanical completion — and can be full-time for a major program. Construction administration hands directly into commissioning and qualification: the same punch list, the same turnover packages, and the same engineer.

Deliverables

  • Submittal & RFI logs and reviews
  • Field observation reports
  • Progress & pay-application verification
  • Change order technical and cost review
  • Shutdown & tie-in plans
  • Punch list & turnover package review

Who this is for

Owners building inside or next to an operating GMP facility; site engineering groups stretched across production support and capital work; owners who want a contractor's progress and invoices independently verified; projects with complex MEP, cleanroom, or process installations.

Why JCS Engineering

We have administered construction for facility expansions, cleanroom buildouts, solvent and utility systems, and equipment installations in operating cGMP plants — where the cost of a shutdown is measured in batches, not days. We know which submittals matter, which RFIs signal a design gap, and how to sequence work so production stays on plan.
