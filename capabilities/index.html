What we do.

Six services covering a capital project from concept through a qualified, operating facility — engaged individually or as an integrated scope.

01

Project Engineering & Management

We take the owner's seat on a capital project: defining scope, driving schedule and budget, coordinating the design firm, contractors, vendors, and quality organization, and holding every party to the intent of the project. Engagements range from a single embedded engineer to a full project team, structured around defined stage gates with clear deliverables at each handoff.

Typical deliverablesProject execution planStage-gate framework & criteriaIntegrated schedule & budget trackingWeekly status & decision logCloseout & lessons learned
02

Conceptual & Detailed Design

We carry engineering design from concept through detailed design: user requirements and basis of design, process flow diagrams and P&IDs, process calculations, equipment and instrument specification, and issued-for-construction packages. The work covers process systems, clean and black utilities, CIP, solvent and fluid-transfer systems, and the cleanroom and HVAC environments around them.

Typical deliverablesURS & basis of designPFDs & P&IDsProcess calculationsEquipment & instrument specificationsDetailed design & IFC packages
03

Construction Administration & Oversight

We manage the submittal and RFI flow, walk the work, verify progress against the schedule and pay applications, and review change orders for scope, cost, and compliance impact. Construction is sequenced around live operations so production isn't collateral damage, and turnover packages are reviewed before anything is accepted.

Typical deliverablesSubmittal & RFI managementField observation reportsProgress & pay-application verificationChange order reviewPunch list & turnover review
04

Commissioning, Qualification & Validation

We plan and execute CQV so systems reach a documented, GMP-ready state on schedule: impact and risk assessments, FAT and SAT, IQ/OQ/PQ protocols and reports, and a traceability matrix that ties every test back to a requirement. Working with your quality unit, the handover package stands up to inspection and your teams can run the system on day one.

Typical deliverablesC&Q plan & impact assessmentsFAT / SAT protocols & executionIQ / OQ / PQ protocols & reportsRequirements traceability matrixTurnover & validation summary
05

Change, Risk & Compliance Management

Every project changes; we make sure change is assessed, priced, approved, and documented rather than absorbed silently. A risk register that is actually reviewed keeps exposure visible to leadership, and document control, traceability, and design justification are built into project governance so the facility is inspection-ready when it is production-ready.

Typical deliverablesChange control procedure & logRisk register & mitigation plansDocument control planDesign qualification supportInspection readiness assessment
06

Process Optimization & Tech Transfer

We support the gap assessment, facility-fit evaluation, and equipment and utility modifications that make a tech transfer succeed, and apply the same lens to lines already in production: throughput, reliability, and yield. After handover we stay through startup and stabilization to close open items and capture what the next project should learn.

Typical deliverablesFacility & equipment fit-gap assessmentProcess & utility demand analysisModification scope & estimateStartup & stabilization supportPerformance review & lessons learned
