Project Engineering & Management

An owner's representative is the engineer on your side of the table: the one party on a capital project whose only interest is the owner's. JCS Engineering fills that seat for pharmaceutical, biotech, and advanced manufacturing owners in North Carolina — defining scope, driving schedule and budget, coordinating the design firm, contractors, vendors, and quality organization, and holding every one of them to the intent of the project.

Conceptual & Detailed Design

JCS Engineering carries engineering design from concept through detailed design for pharmaceutical, biotech, and advanced manufacturing facilities. The work starts with user requirements and a defensible basis of design and ends with construction-ready packages — process flow diagrams, P&IDs, calculations, and equipment and instrument specifications — for process systems and the utilities and environments around them.

Typical deliverablesURS & basis of designBlock flow diagrams & adjacency studiesPFDs & P&IDsProcess, hydraulic & relief calculationsEquipment & instrument specifications and data sheets

Construction Administration & Oversight

Drawings describe intent; construction is where intent meets a jobsite. JCS Engineering provides owner-side construction administration for pharmaceutical, biotech, and advanced manufacturing facilities: managing the submittal and RFI flow, walking the work, verifying progress and pay applications, reviewing change orders, and sequencing construction around operations that cannot stop.

Typical deliverablesSubmittal & RFI logs and reviewsField observation reportsProgress & pay-application verificationChange order technical and cost reviewShutdown & tie-in plans

04

Commissioning, Qualification & Validation

Commissioning, qualification, and validation is where a construction project becomes a production asset — or stalls for months. JCS Engineering plans and executes CQV for pharmaceutical, biotech, and advanced manufacturing systems: impact and risk assessments, FAT and SAT, IQ/OQ/PQ protocols and execution, and the traceability that ties every test back to a requirement, so the handover package stands up to inspection and your teams can run the system on day one.

Typical deliverablesC&Q plan & risk-based test strategySystem impact & component criticality assessmentsRequirements traceability matrixFAT / SAT protocols & executionIQ / OQ / PQ protocols, execution & reports

05

Change, Risk & Compliance Management

Every capital project changes; the question is whether change is assessed, priced, approved, and documented, or absorbed silently until it appears as a schedule slip or a compliance gap. JCS Engineering builds change control, risk management, and GMP documentation discipline into project governance so the facility is inspection-ready when it is production-ready.

Typical deliverablesProject change control procedure & logRisk register & mitigation plansEscalation frameworkDocument control planDesign qualification report

06

Process Optimization & Tech Transfer

Technology transfer is engineering work disguised as paperwork. Moving a process to a new scale, suite, or site means reconciling equipment differences, utility capacities, materials compatibility, cleaning strategies, and room classifications before the process runs where it has not run before. JCS Engineering provides the fit-gap assessment, scale-up engineering, and modifications that make a transfer succeed — and applies the same lens to lines already in production.

Typical deliverablesFacility fit-gap assessment & scored gap listScale-up calculations & critical process parametersProcess & utility demand analysisModification scope, estimate & designStartup & stabilization support
