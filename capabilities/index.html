What we do.

Engineering and project services covering a capital project from the first capacity conversation to a qualified, operating facility — and, when the need is headcount rather than scope, engineers and project managers embedded in your team.

Project Engineering & Owner's Representation

Most owners don't have a spare engineer to run a capital project. JCS Engineering fills that seat: reviewing design, questioning assumptions, driving the schedule, and holding every other party to the intent of the project — with no financial stake in the construction contract. We act as the owner's single point of contact across the architect-engineer, construction manager, equipment vendors, and quality organization, so decisions get made once, on the record, by someone who answers to you.

Typical deliverablesProject execution planWeekly status & decision logBudget and schedule trackingDesign review commentsMeeting leadership & minutesCloseout & lessons learned
02

Capital Project Lifecycle Management

Capital projects fail in the gaps between phases — a basis of design that never made it into the URS, a procurement decision that quietly invalidated the validation plan. Lifecycle management means owning those transitions, not just the phases. We structure projects around defined stage gates with explicit deliverables and go/no-go criteria, so leadership sees a decision at each gate rather than a surprise at the end.

Typical deliverablesStage-gate framework & criteriaIntegrated master scheduleDeliverables registerGate review packagesPhase handover checklists
03

Conceptual & Front-End Design Support

The cheapest change on any project is the one made before drawings exist. Front-end work is where capacity, layout, flows, and utilities get settled — and where an owner's engineer earns the most for the least. We translate operational and quality requirements into a defensible basis of design and user requirement specifications, and pressure-test concepts against constructability, GMP flows, and real budgets.

Typical deliverablesBasis of design (BOD)User requirement specifications (URS)Block flow & adjacency studiesOption comparison & recommendationEstimate review & risk contingency input
04

Construction Administration & Oversight

Drawings describe intent. Construction is where intent meets a jobsite — and where an owner without field presence learns about problems after they're expensive. We manage the submittal and RFI flow, walk the work, track progress against the schedule and pay applications, and coordinate construction around live operations so production isn't collateral damage.

Typical deliverablesSubmittal & RFI logsField observation reportsProgress and pay-app verificationChange order reviewPunch list managementTurnover package review
05

CQV Execution

Commissioning, qualification, and validation is where projects either become production assets or stall for months. It rewards planning that started during design — impact assessments, test strategies, and documentation that traces cleanly back to requirements. We plan and execute CQV from commissioning plan through IQ, OQ, and PQ, working with your quality unit so the handover package stands up to inspection and your teams can run the system on day one.

Typical deliverablesCommissioning & qualification planSystem impact / risk assessmentsFAT / SAT protocols & executionIQ / OQ / PQ protocols and reportsRequirements traceability matrixTurnover & validation summary reports
06

Change Management & Risk Mitigation

Every project changes. The question is whether change is managed deliberately — assessed, priced, approved, and documented — or absorbed silently until it shows up as a schedule slip or a compliance gap. We run a disciplined change process and a risk register that's actually reviewed, so leadership sees exposure early and decisions are made with the full picture.

Typical deliverablesChange control procedureChange log with impact & costRisk register & mitigation plansEscalation frameworkRegular risk reviews
07

Process Optimization & Tech Transfer

Tech transfer is engineering work disguised as paperwork. Equipment differences, utility capacities, cleaning strategies, and room classifications all have to be reconciled before a process runs where it hasn't before. We support the gap assessment, facility-fit evaluation, and equipment and utility modifications that make a transfer succeed, and apply the same lens to optimizing processes already in place.

Typical deliverablesFacility & equipment fit-gap assessmentProcess flow & utility demand analysisModification scope and estimateTransfer execution supportOptimization recommendations
08

Facility Expansion & Master Planning

Expansion decided one project at a time produces facilities that fight themselves: utilities at capacity, flows that cross, and the next project always harder than the last. We develop site and facility master plans that phase growth logically, protect current operations during construction, and give leadership a capital roadmap rather than a series of one-off requests.

Typical deliverablesCapacity & demand modelSite & utility master planPhasing and sequencing strategyOrder-of-magnitude capital roadmap
09

Regulatory & GMP Compliance Readiness

Compliance isn't a phase. Requirements traceability, design documentation, and change records either accumulate cleanly throughout a project or get reconstructed painfully before an inspection. We build GMP thinking into project governance from the start — how documents are controlled, how changes are assessed, how the design is justified — so the facility is inspection-ready when it's production-ready.

Typical deliverablesDocument control planDesign qualification supportTraceability approachInspection readiness assessmentRemediation plans
10

Continuous Improvement & Post-Project Support

Handover isn't the finish line — it's the first day systems run under real conditions. The first months surface the tuning, training gaps, and small fixes that decide whether a project is remembered as a success. We stay engaged through startup and stabilization, close out open items, and capture lessons that make the next project cheaper and faster.

Typical deliverablesStartup & stabilization supportPunch list closurePerformance reviewLessons-learned reportOperational readiness assessment
11

Engineering & Project Management Staff Augmentation

Sometimes the gap isn't expertise — it's headcount. A capital program adds a year or more of engineering and project-management load that your permanent team was never sized for, and a new hire takes months you don't have. JCS Engineering places experienced project engineers, project managers, and CQV leads directly into your organization for the life of a program — working your systems, your procedures, and your reporting lines, under the firm's PE-led accountability.

Typical deliverablesEmbedded project engineer or project managerCQV lead and execution engineersDefined-term or milestone-based staffingReporting through your governanceClean transition at program end
