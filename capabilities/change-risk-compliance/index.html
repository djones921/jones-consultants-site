Change, Risk and Compliance Management for cGMP Capital Projects

Every capital project changes; the question is whether change is assessed, priced, approved, and documented, or absorbed silently until it appears as a schedule slip or a compliance gap. JCS Engineering builds change control, risk management, and GMP documentation discipline into project governance so the facility is inspection-ready when it is production-ready.

Scope of service

  • Change control. A project change procedure with a single approval path; impact assessment of each change on scope, cost, schedule, qualification, and compliance; a change log that survives the project.
  • Risk management. A risk register that is actually reviewed — owners, triggers, mitigations, and dates — with an escalation framework so leadership sees exposure early.
  • Document control. A document control plan for design, construction, and qualification records; version and approval discipline; traceability from requirement to design to test.
  • Design qualification support. Documented review that the design meets the URS and applicable GMP requirements before construction — the foundation for IQ/OQ later.
  • Inspection readiness. Gap assessment of project documentation against regulatory expectations, remediation plans, and support during pre-approval and routine inspections.

How we work

Governance is set up in the first weeks of an engagement and runs for the life of the project: a weekly risk and change review, a decision log, and gate packages that show leadership the current exposure. We work inside your quality system — your change control and document management procedures — rather than imposing a parallel one, and we coordinate with quality assurance so engineering changes and quality records stay aligned.

Deliverables

  • Project change control procedure & log
  • Risk register & mitigation plans
  • Escalation framework
  • Document control plan
  • Design qualification report
  • Inspection readiness assessment & remediation plan

Who this is for

Owners whose project scope is evolving while work is underway; quality units who need engineering changes assessed for GMP impact; sites preparing for pre-approval inspection of a new facility; multi-vendor programs that need one approval path for change.

Why JCS Engineering

We have run change control and risk management on programs from targeted equipment upgrades to a $25M multi-product facility conversion, and we have prepared and reviewed the documentation that inspectors ask for — because we wrote it during the project rather than reconstructing it afterward.
