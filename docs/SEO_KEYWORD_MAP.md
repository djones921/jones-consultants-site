# Keyword Map — jcseng.com

**Method (zero-cost):** derived from the six services, standard life-sciences engineering search phrasing, Google autocomplete and "People also ask" patterns for the service nouns (owner's representative, CQV, tech transfer, P&ID, cGMP facility design), Google Business Profile category names, and the vocabulary buyers actually use in RFPs (owner's engineer, C&Q, IQ/OQ/PQ, basis of design, URS, FAT/SAT). Volumes are not shown because no paid tool was used; ordering within each service reflects expected commercial value, not volume.

**Positioning decision:** JCS competes for *specialist* queries (pharma/biotech capital projects, CQV, owner's rep, tech transfer) with a *local* modifier (Raleigh, Research Triangle, North Carolina, Holly Springs, Clayton, Durham) — not for generic "engineering firm" queries dominated by national firms. Every target page pairs one specialist noun with one geography or one buyer situation.

**Geography set** (used in copy, `areaServed`, and the service-area page): Raleigh · Research Triangle Park (RTP) · Durham · Cary · Holly Springs · Clayton · Wake County · Johnston County · Sanford · Wilson · Greenville · North Carolina (statewide, under NC firm license P-3451). ## Competitors — from the owner's SERP survey, 2026-09-08 (incognito, Raleigh/Knightdale)

Fourteen target phrases were searched. JCS did not appear for any — the day-one baseline. Three structural findings shaped the follow-up changes:

1. **AI Overviews lead every specialist query.** Firms named in them share three traits: a dedicated service page, a city attached to the description ("based in Cary, NC", "office in Raleigh"), and third-party confirmation (Indeed postings, LinkedIn, directories). Site-side traits are now in place; citations remain the gap.
2. **A local Places pack appears for "pharmaceutical engineering consultant Raleigh NC" and "pharma engineering firm Raleigh NC"**, populated by Business Profiles in the *Engineering consultant* category — including firms with two or zero reviews. This is the most winnable placement; reviews decide order within it.
3. **"Engineer" as the head noun pulls job-search results** (Jobs tab, salary "People also ask"). Titles and H1s now use *firm / services / consultants*.
