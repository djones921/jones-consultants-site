# Keyword Map — jcseng.com

**Method (zero-cost):** derived from the six services, standard life-sciences engineering search phrasing, Google autocomplete and "People also ask" patterns for the service nouns (owner's representative, CQV, tech transfer, P&ID, cGMP facility design), Google Business Profile category names, and the vocabulary buyers actually use in RFPs (owner's engineer, C&Q, IQ/OQ/PQ, basis of design, URS, FAT/SAT). Volumes are not shown because no paid tool was used; ordering within each service reflects expected commercial value, not volume.

**Positioning decision:** JCS competes for *specialist* queries (pharma/biotech capital projects, CQV, owner's rep, tech transfer) with a *local* modifier (Raleigh, Research Triangle, North Carolina, Holly Springs, Clayton, Durham) — not for generic "engineering firm" queries dominated by national firms. Every target page pairs one specialist noun with one geography or one buyer situation.

**Geography set** (used in copy, `areaServed`, and the service-area page): Raleigh · Research Triangle Park (RTP) · Durham · Cary · Holly Springs · Clayton · Wake County · Johnston County · Sanford · Wilson · Greenville · North Carolina (statewide, under NC firm license P-3451). Knightdale, Wendell, Zebulon, Garner, Apex, Wake Forest appear in the areaServed list as Wake County municipalities **[VERIFY office location]**.
