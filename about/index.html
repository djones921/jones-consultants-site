Engineering judgment, on your side of the table.

JCS Engineering PLLC is a Raleigh-based project engineering and owner's representation practice serving pharmaceutical, biotech, and advanced manufacturing clients.

The Firm

Built to sit on the owner's side.

We exist because most owners don't have a spare engineer to run a capital project — and the parties who do have engineers on site each have their own contract to protect.

JCS Engineering guides projects from early concept through design, construction, CQV, and ongoing optimization, ensuring that every investment delivers lasting value. We bring hands-on GMP operations experience to the work, which shows up in the way we sequence construction around production, plan qualification from the first design review, and write things down so the record survives the project.

We're deliberately small. That means the engineer you meet in the first conversation is the engineer on the project — and the engagement scales to what the project needs rather than to what a bench of staff needs to stay busy.
